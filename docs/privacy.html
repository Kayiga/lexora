Lexora Privacy Policy

Voice Dictation & Transcription

Last updated: May 2026
The short version

Lexora does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on behalf of the developer. Your voice and transcripts never leave your device or your private iCloud account.

📦 What Lexora collects

Nothing. Lexora does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on behalf of the developer. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no servers we operate that receive your data.

📱 Where your data lives

All data created by Lexora — transcripts, vocabulary, voice profile, session history — is stored in exactly two places:

  • On your device in local app storage
  • In your private iCloud account via CloudKit (encrypted end-to-end; only you can access it)

No data is sent to any server operated by Lexora or its developer.

🎙️ Microphone & speech recognition

Lexora records your microphone and converts speech to text using Apple's on-device Speech Recognition framework (SFSpeechRecognizer).

  • When on-device recognition is available for your language, audio is processed entirely on your iPhone — audio is never transmitted anywhere.
  • When on-device recognition is unavailable for a given language, Apple may process audio on Apple's own servers under Apple's Privacy Policy. Lexora has no control over this and does not receive the audio.

🤖 Optional AI features (Premium)

Lexora Premium includes an optional AI feature that sends transcript text (not audio) to OpenAI's API to generate summaries, action items, and follow-up suggestions.

  • Opt-in only — activates only when you explicitly tap "Generate AI Insights"
  • Uses your own OpenAI API key, which you enter in Settings
  • Your API key is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device — never transmitted to the developer or any third party other than OpenAI
  • When text is sent to OpenAI, it is governed by OpenAI's Privacy Policy

⌨️ Keyboard extension

The LexoraKeyboard extension requires "Allow Full Access" to share your learned vocabulary and voice profile with the main app. This sharing happens through an Apple App Group — a local, on-device channel. No data leaves your device through the keyboard extension.

👤 Contacts (optional)

If you grant Contacts access, Lexora reads contact names to improve recognition of proper nouns. Contact data is never stored, transmitted, or processed beyond the session in which it is used.

📊 Analytics & tracking

Lexora contains no third-party analytics SDK, no advertising framework, and no tracking pixels. There is no usage telemetry of any kind sent to the developer.

👶 Children

Lexora is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

🔄 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the update will be noted with a new "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
