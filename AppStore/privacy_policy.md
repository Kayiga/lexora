# Lexora Privacy Policy

*Last updated: May 2026*

---

## What Lexora collects

**Nothing.** Lexora does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on behalf of the developer.

---

## How your data is stored

All data created by Lexora — transcripts, vocabulary, voice profile, session history — is stored:

- **On your device** in local app storage
- **In your private iCloud account** via CloudKit (encrypted, only you can access it)

No data is sent to any server operated by the developer.

---

## Microphone and speech recognition

Lexora records your microphone and converts speech to text using **Apple's on-device Speech Recognition framework** (SFSpeechRecognizer). When on-device recognition is available for your language, audio is processed entirely on your iPhone and never transmitted anywhere.

When on-device recognition is unavailable for a given language, Apple may process audio on its servers under Apple's own privacy policy. Lexora has no control over this and does not receive the audio.

---

## Optional AI features

Lexora Premium includes an optional AI feature that sends transcript *text* (not audio) to **OpenAI's API** to generate summaries, action items, and follow-up suggestions.

- This feature is **opt-in** — it only activates when you explicitly tap "Generate AI Insights"
- It uses **your own OpenAI API key**, which you enter in Settings
- Your API key is stored in the **iOS Keychain** on your device — it is never transmitted to the developer or any third party other than OpenAI
- When text is sent to OpenAI, it is governed by **OpenAI's privacy policy**: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy

---

## Keyboard extension

The LexoraKeyboard extension requires "Allow Full Access" to share your learned vocabulary and voice profile with the main app. This sharing happens through an **Apple App Group** — a local, on-device channel. No data leaves your device through the keyboard extension.

---

## Contacts (optional)

If you grant Contacts access, Lexora reads contact names to improve recognition of proper nouns. Contact data is never stored, transmitted, or processed beyond the session in which it is used.

---

## Analytics and tracking

Lexora contains **no third-party analytics SDK**, no advertising framework, and no tracking pixels. There is no usage telemetry of any kind.

---

## Children

Lexora is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

---

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the update will be noted with a new "Last updated" date.
